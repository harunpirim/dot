Consumption Must Flow

[Consumption Must Flow](https://www.frontiermag.net/consumption-must-flow/)

The advent of AI, particularly reasoning-capable models, threatens to disrupt the traditional economic feedback loop by automating knowledge work and severing the wage flow that drives consumption. While some argue that new industries will emerge, AI’s ability to replicate human capabilities across various domains suggests that job displacement will be widespread and potentially permanent. To mitigate the economic consequences, policymakers may need to consider radical solutions like Universal Basic Income, which would provide a guaranteed stipend to ensure a stable base of consumption and prevent systemic economic collapse.
